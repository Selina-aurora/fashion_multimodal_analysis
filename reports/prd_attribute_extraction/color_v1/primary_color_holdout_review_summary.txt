Important
---------
- Accuracy excludes rows with no clearly dominant single color.
- This avoids forcing a questionable GT label onto genuinely multicolor garments.
- The current taxonomy maps cream/ivory to beige and magenta to pink.
- Main observed errors are isolated color confusions plus one target-contamination case.
- CLIP scores remain candidate-relative similarities, not calibrated probabilities.

Interpretation
--------------
- The primary-color baseline is provisionally strong enough to keep as a baseline.
- Remaining uncertainty is dominated by multicolor garments and close color boundaries.
- Secondary/multi-color extraction should be a separate later extension, not forced into this single-primary-color score.
