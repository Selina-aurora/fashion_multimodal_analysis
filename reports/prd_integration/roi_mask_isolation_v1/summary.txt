Decision rule
-------------
- If C is much worse than D, predicted mask quality is the stronger error source.
- If D is much worse than C, bbox crop quality is the stronger error source.
- If both C and D are degraded, both components contribute or interact.
- Compare sleeve_length and neckline first; they are the main local-structure attributes.
